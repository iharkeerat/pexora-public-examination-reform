## Releasing additional source documents

Before adding any original document:

1. Reconcile the version with the 2026 submission of record.
2. Check copyright and permission for third-party material.
3. Remove direct and indirect personal identifiers securely, including metadata and hidden PDF layers.
4. Separate historical submitted text from later revisions.
5. Verify selectable text, hyperlinks and embedded objects after redaction.
6. Reassess whether the detailed legal/security material should be public.

A public repository does **not** imply governmental, legal, academic or scientific validation.
