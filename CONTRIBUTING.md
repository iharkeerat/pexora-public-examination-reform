# Review and feedback

This project welcomes **substantive research critique**, including correction of legal references, constitutional competence analysis, psychometric concerns, accessibility constraints, fiscal realism, privacy and cybersecurity questions, and implementation risks.

Please focus on verifiable, scoped comments and cite the relevant [executive overview](executive-summary/overview.md), proposed framework document or methodological note. A 15-page public-ready Executive Legislative Brief has been prepared but is not yet uploaded here.

Do **not** post personal contact details, examination credentials, private correspondence, confidential government documents, claims about identifiable exam candidates, or third-party material without permission.

Corrections should distinguish:

- An error in the publicly released summary.
- A disputed provision in the original 2026 submission.
- A new proposal or later amendment.

No contribution should be taken as legal, scientific or governmental approval.
