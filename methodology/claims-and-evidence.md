## Evidence classes

- **Primary proposed text** establishes what the author proposes.
- **Official correspondence** establishes only what a source expressly states, once verified.
- **Empirical implementation data** would be required to demonstrate practical impact.
- **Independent expert review** would be needed to assess legal, psychometric, fiscal and operational feasibility.

No unredacted contact details, private submissions or original correspondence are included in the public release.
