# Methodology and authorship disclosure

## Nature of the project

PEXORA is **normative legal, institutional and policy-design research**, not an experimental proof of effectiveness. It combines problem framing, proposed constitutional and statutory drafting, comparative domain reasoning, administrative-process mapping, scientific-assurance requirements and an implementation-oriented risk register.

The submitted volume includes a constitutional amendment proposal, a principal Bill, policy frameworks, proposed rules and regulations, a postgraduate-pathways framework and a provisional legal/numerical validation register. The public companion repository provides an excerpted primary brief and explanatory research materials, not a complete legal submission.

## Working approach represented by the documents

1. Identify candidate-facing examination failure modes, including integrity, scientific quality, accessibility, response custody, process delay and ineffective remedy.
2. Separate actor responsibilities, evidence duties, safeguards, independent review and remedies.
3. Specify alternative routes and protected powers rather than assuming one institution can displace all others.
4. Design staged implementation with readiness gates, domain-specialist review and explicit no-go/rollback options.
5. Maintain a provisional register of legal-enabling questions, numerical assumptions and unresolved risks.

## Review not yet performed by this repository

This release does **not** assert independent counsel review, accepted academic peer review, new psychometric field data, certified security testing, completed feasibility costing, implementation or government adoption. It does not contain a validated comparative impact study.

## Use of artificial intelligence and digital tools

The submitted master volume contains an explicit **Disclosure on the Use of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Tools** (introductory dossier, printed p. 7). It describes AI as an aid in drafting and production, with human direction and responsibility. This companion documentation was also prepared with AI assistance and reviewed against the accessible source for scope and major claims. Descriptions should not be read as unassisted human-only drafting.

## Review priorities

Constitutional and legislative competence; delegated authority; independent assessment science; accommodation and accessibility; financial assumptions; cybersecurity and privacy; administrative capacity; appellate due process; federal and constitutional boundaries; cost and failure-mode analysis.
