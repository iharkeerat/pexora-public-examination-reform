# Executive overview

**PEXORA: A National Architecture for Fair, Secure, Scientifically Assured and Candidate-Centred Public Examinations** is a citizen-authored reform proposal submitted in 2026. It is not an enacted statute or an approved government programme.

## Problem addressed

High-stakes examination systems can fail through material compromise, inaccessible procedures, defective or inequivalent questions, unclear scoring, response-record errors, vendor or insider failures, and remedies that arrive too late. Preventing leaks is necessary but insufficient: a secure examination must also justify its score interpretations and consequential uses.

## Proposed response

1. **Separate decision functions** between operations, scientific assurance, candidate redress, appeals and competent investigation.
2. **Protect candidates throughout the lifecycle** from registration through results, challenge, correction, restoration and lawful remedy.
3. **Require scientific assurance** for specified score uses, not merely the absence of reported security incidents.
4. **Preserve constitutional and federal boundaries** and protected State, commission, university and judicial roles.
5. **Use evidence-gated pilots** with go / limited-go / no-go decisions and lawful continuity protections.

## Decision sought in the original brief

The author requested competent classification, multidisciplinary governmental review, specialist assessment of constitutional, psychometric, cybersecurity, accessibility and fiscal questions, consultation with relevant institutions and candidate groups, and limited studies or pilots where justified. The proposal **does not** seek enactment without review.

## Evidence and release status

The primary master document records the 2026 submission date and the architecture proposed. It does not prove governmental acceptance, legislative feasibility, independent scientific validation, or adoption.

A redacted 15-page Executive Legislative Brief has been prepared separately for public release but **is not yet hosted in this GitHub repository**.
