# Scope, limitations and non-claims

1. **No government sponsorship or adoption.** PEXORA is an independent citizen-authored proposal. Forwarding and submission do not mean approval.
2. **No legal opinion.** Constitutional competence, ratification, federal boundaries, delegated powers, tribunal design and remedies require qualified legal review.
3. **No psychometric certification.** Validity, reliability, equating, accessibility and permitted score uses are *proposed assurance domains*, not validated outcomes here.
4. **No demonstrated efficacy.** The proposal has not been established as reducing real-world leaks, unfair scores, exclusion, costs or delays in a field trial.
5. **No final fiscal modelling.** Funding, implementation capacity and numerical parameters remain provisional.
6. **No complete publication of primary source.** The full source volume is intentionally withheld from this repository pending document-level privacy and version review.
7. **AI-assisted drafting disclosed.** Human authorship of project direction does not mean every sentence was written without computational assistance.
8. **No authority over protected institutions.** Proposed responsibilities cannot be taken as existing powers to direct States, universities, commissions or courts.

For evidence boundaries and sources, see [Claims and Evidence](methodology/claims-and-evidence.md).
