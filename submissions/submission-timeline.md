## Interpreting the record

A government office **receiving, forwarding, routing or acknowledging** a citizen's proposal does not signify endorsement, legislative sponsorship, expert validation, adoption, or an obligation to implement it.

Any future entry should preserve:

- The exact communication date.
- The issuing authority and intended recipient.
- The specific type of action (*acknowledged*, *forwarded*, *requested clarification*, *accepted for review*, etc.).
- A redacted document or citation where publication is appropriate.
- A note separating the communication from any substantive policy decision.

For now, the public timeline is deliberately conservative. No unredacted correspondence has been released.
