# Federal boundaries and evidence-gated implementation

## Not a general central takeover

The proposal explicitly preserves constitutional and lawful functions of State institutions, Public Service Commissions, universities, professional bodies and courts. It distinguishes examination-integrity requirements from substantive recruitment, curricula, admissions and professional judgments. The proposed constitutional route is **not** assumed to exist already.

## Two phases contemplated by the brief

- **Before any required constitutional commencement:** operate only within existing Parliamentary competence or other lawful routes, voluntary agreements, valid State powers and protected pilots.
- **Following any lawful constitutional route:** minimum safeguards may be considered in the defined area, while protected State functions remain protected.

The legal correctness of this architecture **has not been independently verified**.

## Evidence gates before deployment

The longer submission describes a progression from legal and institutional baseline, fiscal and capacity baseline, protected pilot design and independent evaluation to an examination-family-specific *go / limited-go / no-go* decision. It calls for parallel continuity, monitoring and rollback if critical conditions fail.

Evaluation domains include:

- Legal authority and role separation.
- Psychometric validity, reliability and meaningful score use.
- Accessibility, language and response-mode comparability.
- Security, operational resilience and evidence preservation.
- Candidate burden, public cost, State capacity and continuity.

The proposal treats a **no-go decision** as a legitimate outcome, not a failed publicity exercise.

**Sources:** Executive Legislative Brief pp. 9, 12–14; submitted master introductory dossier, Sections 10–11 (printed pp. 27–30). These are design intentions, not field-trial findings.
