# Ten proposed flagship reforms

Condensed from the original submission's introductory dossier, Section 6, printed p. 21. These are **design proposals**, not implemented measures.

1. **Reduce advance plaintext exposure** of examination material in the operational chain.
2. **Low-exposure authorised activation** supported by readiness, fallback and accessibility safeguards.
3. **Split critical privileges** so no individual or account controls the entire sensitive process.
4. **Candidate-verifiable response custody** with authoritative records, receipts and continuity.
5. **Independent scientific certification** of assessment quality, fairness and permitted score use.
6. **Candidate Rights Charter** covering lawful access, information, accommodation, correction and remedy.
7. **Secure human identity alternatives** when electronic verification fails.
8. **Restoration and compensation mechanisms** for eligible failures affecting participation and opportunity.
9. **Independent grievance and appeal pathways** to avoid the operator being the sole final reviewer.
10. **Protected pilots and no-go outcomes** before any irreversible replacement.

### Important boundary

The existence of a proposed safeguard does not establish its feasibility or effectiveness. Operational thresholds, statutory duties, scientific standards and remedies depend on legal authority, available evidence and stakeholder scrutiny. This summary is **not a technical implementation manual**.
