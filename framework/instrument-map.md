## Instrument hierarchy

The constitutional proposal, principal Bill, policy frameworks, central rules, regulations and validation register occupy **different legal and drafting roles**. The inclusion of a document titled 'Bill', 'Rules', or 'Regulations' does not make it law. Enabling powers, delegated scope, legislative competence, constitutional procedure and financial assumptions require specialist review.

## Review prerequisites

- Constitution and federal competence, especially State and commission functions.
- Drafting authority, delegated powers, tribunal design, judicial review and remedies.
- Psychometric methods, permitted score interpretation, language comparability and evidence quality.
- Candidate accessibility, data governance, security, procurement, cost and capacity.
- Pilots, independent evaluation, decision gates and transition continuity.
